# Artificial Intelligence and Big Data at USP

I completed the Artificial Intelligence and Big Data program at the University of São Paulo's
Institute of Mathematics and Computer Sciences (ICMC/USP) in 2023.

## Final project

My final project explored how to simulate multi-agent, turn-based board games using reinforcement
learning. The work used a Value-Decomposition Network (VDN) and the Gymnasium API to model agents that
learn to cooperate in a shared environment.

The project was advised by Prof. Dr. Fernando Santos Osório and presented to ICMC/USP's Department of
Computer Science as part of the requirements for the program's specialist title.
